# disable-user): package names, one per line. Google APIs and Play Store images have them;
# what an image lacks is skipped. Left on, as an app reaches them: Chrome (links, custom tabs,
# sign-in pages), the Play Store (billing, in-app review and updates, Play Integrity), Play
# services (push, maps, sign-in, location), the keyboard, Messages, the phone, contacts,
# calendar, clock, camera, Maps and files apps (an app's intents open them), and the text to
# speech engine.
