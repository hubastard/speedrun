# The iOS simulator's background services that `speedrun slim` turns off: launchd labels, one
# per line. From SimSlim (MIT License, LICENSE here; https://github.com/MobAI-App/simslim),
# grouped as it groups them, less what an app reaches through a public framework, which stays
# on: push (apsd), universal links (swcd), the photo library (assetsd), contacts, calendars
# and reminders, HealthKit (healthd), HomeKit (homed), StoreKit's products (storekitd,
# itunesstored), Apple Pay (passd, financed), DeviceCheck, Sign in with Apple (akd), CloudKit
# and iCloud documents and keys (cloudd, bird, syncdefaultsd), Spotlight's searchd (the
# document picker's Recents), Safari's history and Safe Browsing and web privacy (in-app
# browsers), CallKit, Live Activities, ad attribution (adprivacyd) and map snapshots. The App
# Store's engagement services (AMS) are off: in-app purchases cannot be made, and they are
# busy for minutes after a first boot.
